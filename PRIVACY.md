# mailreader: privacy policy

Last updated 12 October 2026.

mailreader is a personal program used only by its author, Xiaozhen Liu, with his own mail
accounts. See the [home page](README.md) for what it is.

## What it accesses

With the permission `gmail.readonly`, mailreader reads the messages of the Google account that
signed in: their headers, their text, and the names of their attachments. It cannot send,
change or delete anything.

## Where the data goes

mailreader runs only on the account owner's computer. Messages are read when the owner's AI
assistant asks for them and are handed to that assistant on the same computer. The assistant
processes them under its own provider's terms, as it does everything else the owner gives it.
mailreader itself has no server, and sends nothing to its author or to anyone else. It does
not sell data, show advertising, or use data to train models.

## What it stores

mailreader keeps no copy of any message. The sign-in token is stored in the macOS Keychain of
the owner's computer and nowhere else.

## Removing access

Access ends when the owner runs `mailreader logout`, or removes mailreader at
<https://myaccount.google.com/permissions>.

## Google's rules

mailreader's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the
[Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy),
including the Limited Use requirements.
